✳ LITTLE WONDERS

THE SOUNDS OF OUR LITTLE WORLD

Audio credits

All audio is bundled with this storybook. No device speech service is used.

Every English or Mandarin line uses the selected fictional Zoey / 03-curious reference from the local voice lab. The exact reference conditions Qwen3-TTS Base during development; only the resulting AAC recordings run in the browser. The voice profile, reference checksum, pinned model revisions, and generation tools are versioned with the project. The owl hoot, tiger growl, and the added hippo, rhino, otter, panda, kangaroo, penguin, crocodile, flamingo, gorilla, fox, deer, rabbit, horse, and cow calls are original, softly crafted animal-inspired effects. They are illustrative effects rather than wildlife field recordings. The monkey sound is a playful human imitation, not a zoological recording.

Voice tools: Qwen3-TTS and MLX Audio, under their respective Apache 2.0 and MIT licenses. The selected reference was generated from a fictional character description. No human reference recording was used.

Recorded calls were converted to browser-compatible MP3 or AAC. Storybook clips combine the English name, a pause, and up to four seconds of the call. Call volume is limited and the ends are faded. These audio edits retain the source licenses listed above. Original downloadable call files are included beside the storybook clips.

The Mandarin planting story uses original words and artwork; no song recording or melody is included. Vector lifecycle illustrations were adapted from the supplied butterfly-adventure.html reference. Zoey, the panorama scenery, and animal illustrations are drawn in SVG for this implementation.
