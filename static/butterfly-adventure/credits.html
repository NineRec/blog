THE SOUNDS OF OUR LITTLE WORLD

Audio credits

All audio is bundled with this storybook. No device speech service is used.

English narration and animal names were rendered to audio files during development using the macOS Samantha voice. The owl hoot and tiger growl are original, softly synthesized sound effects. The monkey sound is a playful human imitation, not a zoological recording.

  • Elephant: Elephant voice – trumpeting, by தகவலுழவன் / Info-farmer. CC0 1.0.
  • Lion: Lion raring-sound1TamilNadu178, by த*உழவன் / Info-farmer. Released into the public domain by its author.
  • Giraffe: Giraffe grunt, by Anton Baotic, Florian Sicks and Angela S. Stoeger. CC BY-SA 4.0. The bundled call and its edited portion in giraffe.m4a are distributed under the same license.
  • Zebra: Zebra Whinny, recorded by Peet J van Eeden, extracted and cleaned by AntumDeluge. CC BY 4.0.
  • Monkey: Monkey Sounds, performed and edited by AntumDeluge. The download includes a CC BY 3.0 license, which is used here; the source page also offers CC0.
  • Bear: Bear growl, recorded by Shizhao. CC BY 3.0.
  • Duck: Mallard, XC62258, recorded by Jonathon Jongsma. CC BY-SA 3.0. The bundled duck call and its edited portion in duck.m4a retain this license.
  • Frog: Ribbit Frog Sounds, by EZduzziteh. CC0 1.0.

Recorded calls were converted to browser-compatible MP3 or AAC. Storybook clips combine the English name, a pause, and up to four seconds of the call. Call volume is limited and the ends are faded. These audio edits retain the source licenses listed above. Original downloadable call files are included beside the storybook clips.

Vector lifecycle illustrations were adapted from the supplied butterfly-adventure.html reference. Zoey, the panorama scenery, and animal illustrations are drawn in SVG for this implementation.
