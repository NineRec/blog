A note for grown-ups

Explore at your child's pace. Tap Zoey to say hello. The garden follows egg → caterpillar → chrysalis → butterfly; the animations show a process that takes days or weeks in nature. At the zoo, tap each animal to hear its English name and call. Under the sea, tap each friend to watch it swim away and return. Swipe either panorama in any direction, move the scene slider, or use the arrow buttons. All voices and sounds come with this book. Turn the device volume up to listen.

Artwork is animated SVG. Monkey chatter is a playful human imitation; the owl hoot and tiger growl are crafted sound effects. The other calls include recordings and a contributed frog sound effect. Audio credits & licenses.
